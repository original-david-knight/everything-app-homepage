Everything App

A personal production tool operated by David Knight.

Everything App is David Knight's own tool for running his projects. One of its parts plans, writes and builds short educational videos, publishes them to his YouTube channel, and reports how they perform. It is not offered to the public; only its operator signs in.

YouTube features

The app uses YouTube API Services to work with the channel David's Amazing Educational Videos:

  • Upload finished videos with their titles, descriptions, captions and thumbnails, and keep the channel's playlists in order.
  • Read the channel's video list and YouTube Analytics numbers once a day, and show them in the app's dashboards.

By using these features you agree to be bound by the YouTube Terms of Service. How the app handles YouTube data, how to have it deleted, and how to revoke access are described in the Privacy Policy.
